QuantiCool Dielectric Fluid Reference Matrix

When speaking with BESS manufacturers, explain the fundamental advantage of dielectric fluids for utility battery packs:

QuantiCool-Immersion
Synthetic Ester Direct Cell Immersion Fluid
  • Kinematic Viscosity: 18 cSt @ 40°C
  • Breakdown Voltage: >75 kV
  • Flash Point: >260°C (High safety margin)
  • Biodegradable & non-toxic
  • Direct cell contact; suppresses runaway propagation
QuantiCool-DLC
Low Viscosity Dielectric for Cold Plates
  • Kinematic Viscosity: 5.5 cSt @ 40°C
  • Pour Point: <-50°C
  • Superior pump efficiency in sub-zero ambient
  • Compatible with LFP & NMC pouch/prismatic cells
  • Low dielectric dissipation factor
Outreach Triage Tip: If an OEM says their BESS container is "Liquid Cooled", ask: "Does your cooling loop run glycol plates under the modules, or are the cells directly bathed in fluid?" If cold-plate, inquire whether they are evaluating immersion for their next-generation higher C-rate products.
